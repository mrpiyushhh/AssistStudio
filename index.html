    03How we work

    A clear path from
    idea to launch.

    Nine deliberate stages. Each one builds on the last so we stay aligned, move with purpose, and deliver outcomes you can measure.

    1. 1 Discovery Call
    2. 2 Requirement Gathering
    3. 3 Scope and Effort Estimation
    4. 4 Proposal Sign-off
    5. 5 Project Kick-Off
    6. 6 Development
    7. 7 Quality Analysis
    8. 8 UAT Handover
    9. 9 Go Live

    Stage 01

    Discovery Call

    A focused conversation to understand your goals, constraints, and what success looks like. We listen first, ask sharp questions, and assess whether we are the right fit to take this further.

    Outcome: Shared clarity on the problem space, priorities, and next steps — with no obligation to proceed.

    Stage 02

    Requirement Gathering

    We dig into workflows, stakeholders, data, and technical constraints. Workshops and structured interviews turn vague needs into concrete requirements that guide every decision downstream.

    Outcome: A clear requirements baseline — user needs, must-haves, constraints, and success criteria documented and agreed.

    Stage 03

    Scope and Effort Estimation

    We translate requirements into a realistic scope, break work into phases, and estimate effort with transparent assumptions. Trade-offs are surfaced early so nothing is left to chance.

    Outcome: A scoped plan with effort ranges, timeline options, and clear boundaries on what is in and out of the first release.

    Stage 04

    Proposal Sign-off

    You receive a clear proposal covering approach, deliverables, timeline, and commercial terms. We refine together until both sides are confident and aligned before any build begins.

    Outcome: Signed agreement on scope, investment, milestones, and responsibilities — a solid foundation for the project.

    Stage 05

    Project Kick-Off

    Teams meet, tools are set up, and the working rhythm is established. Roles, communication channels, and the first sprint goals are locked so everyone starts with the same picture.

    Outcome: An operational project environment — channels live, backlog ready, and the first delivery cycle underway.

    Stage 06

    Development

    We build in iterative cycles with regular demos and feedback loops. AI, software, and integrations are developed against the agreed requirements, with progress visible at every stage.

    Outcome: Working software that evolves with your feedback — features land in priority order, not as a big reveal at the end.

    Stage 07

    Quality Analysis

    Rigorous testing across functionality, performance, edge cases, and security. Issues are found and fixed before they reach your users, so the product is stable when it matters.

    Outcome: A verified build that meets quality bars for reliability, performance, and readiness for user acceptance.

    Stage 08

    UAT Handover

    You validate the solution against real scenarios and acceptance criteria. We support the review, capture feedback, and close remaining gaps so sign-off is confident and complete.

    Outcome: Formal acceptance that the solution does what was agreed — ready for production with no open blockers.

    Stage 09

    Go Live

    We deploy to production, monitor the rollout, and support the transition. Launch is planned, controlled, and backed by a clear support path so day one is stable and day two is better.

    Outcome: A live product in your environment — monitored, supported, and ready to deliver value from day one.
